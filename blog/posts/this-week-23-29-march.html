My Week 23 - 29 March

This has been a productive week for me online, whislt I have been particularly fiddly with the website, I am in a somewhat happy place with it. I really now just need to get down to writing more - recording my thoughts.

WEBSITE

I ended last week well by wrangling with webmentions, a headache until it works. I have subsequently added more of a comment box at the bottom of each blog-type post where people can reply with webmentions form their website, mastodon or wherever they can send a webmention from really. It was a technical thing I was interested in doing because it felt messy and I could get down and dirty with it. There are three buttons above the comments box, back home and archive. self explanitory and felt it right to get that all sorted.

I adopted an idea from from sircmpwn and added their Openring 'articles from blogs I follow' to my home page, their hard work on the code and my design over it. It's a great way to show your appreciation for others and hopefully give them new views. I added a more posts elements to my blog posts too; feels like a good way to tie them up together.

When I get more posts and tags it can maybe then change to a related posts box. I tweaked my about page to include my Fedivrse socials on it and I added a last played function on the right sidebar, the information is currently inside what looks like an iPod classic, it feels kitsch. It is linked up to my Listenbrainz account. I have removed the guestbook and replaced it with a Now Listening page, It's a way for me to log my listens intentionally. Especially the Listenbrainz weekly exploration playlists. I half expected it to load maybe one or two or to have to play a few songs and see if it worked but it added 90+ songs, I have set it to remove duplicates from being counted. I'm not sure if it will stay, it's likely but maybe in a different form - the important thing is that it works. I had a fairly productive week writing....well this is now my third blog post this week, I have added a changelog and updated my now page.

Elsewhere on the web, I've wrangled Brid.gy to some degree, I also signed up for bookwyrm. Reading doesn't come naturally but I have notions here and there, I also have an evergrowning list of titles to read.

OTHER MEDIA

Not watched major amounts of TV this week, I caught the Wales World Cup qualifer against Bosnia and Herzegovina. It was a tough loss for the Welsh - they had the match in the bag for so long but the Bosnians found a way to take it to penalties and get the win. It's weird being Scottish, I've been used to us for majority of my life either losing in the playoffs or being hopeless in our group. It felt weird to finish top of our group and only need six matches to qualify. If Iraq manage it (for football's sake it would be a wonderful story). They would have had played something like 21 matches to make the 2026 World Cup.

Been keeping up with the reboot of Scrubs - can't wait for the next series of Will Trent to air here. I have started watching Sopranos again. Fell asleep watching the Park Chan-Wook directed movie 'No Other Choice' this was my fault and not the film, I started watching it later than I should have. Parts of it feel overly long at the beginning but I love Park Chan-Wook's work so I will definitely need to reschedule and try again.

I haven't had much chance to game this week.

I started reading tower by Bae Myung-Hoon. A science fiction novel about a nation state of 500,000 people living inside an Empire State type tower block. short interconnected stories inside one larger narrative.
