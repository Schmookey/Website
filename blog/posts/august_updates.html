August Updates

UPDATED Sun 12 Aug 2026 20:45
2026-08-12 20:45 BST
⏱ – min read 💬 0 mentions ❤️ 0 🔁 0 🔗 0 replies
Hello! I fear it's been a little while, I have updated my NOW page but aside from that I haven't really been online. I tried to write something for the World Cup but it was more waffle than I could handle to post lol. I have been keeping myself under wraps lately. I had a bit of a clarity moment which has sent me on a path to losing some weight, which is no bad thing. 2 and a half stone so far. I have however been finding it difficult to really start things, I am slowly reading Automatic Noodle by Annalee Newitz, It's a fun novelette about robots picking up the pieces following a war. It's a good read but I'm just being slow lately. I've been building up my music collection adding Aaron-Lee Tasjan's latest album Get Over It, Underdog a notable highlight along with Friends in Dark Places by The Black Drumset. I'm currently writing this vibing to an eclectic playlist of Coba-U, Kino and the aforementioned Aaron-Lee Tasjan. My gaming collection has grown significantly, There was a deal to get the Yakuza games 0 through 6 on GOG with a steep discount, as much as I own them already I now have DRM free versions for my computer, I also got Disco Elysium and Turok 1 - 3 I also bought some more DVD's The Wayne's World Collection, Band of Brothers, Teachers boxset, Yes Minister (Prime Minister) oh and Highlander 5 : The Soure. Yes I know but I am totally pilled on the franchise because of the series and what can I say? I do like a bad movie from time to time. I watched the BBC series Ann Droid, great cast including a cameo by Danny John-Jules....Made me smile so hard when I saw him on the show. In terms of movies, I recently watched Lars and the Real Girl. It wasn't what I expected but it was nice to see a movie about a community coming together to help someone. I also watched a German movie called BLOODSUCKERS, Set in the 1920's what if Marx' work was to be taken literally and aristocrats were actual vampires? My local football team made it to the Fifth tier this season, I've been saying I'll go see them but who knows. I have been slowly writing a novel(ette!?), I don't know if it will be any good honestly but I have an idea I need to get out of my head so I'm doing that at a snails pace.
