Linux EPUB Reader is a privacy-focused desktop application designed for reading DRM-free ebooks, listening to audiobooks and playing locally stored music.

+ STRENGTHS

  • Entirely offline
  • Saved reading positions
  • Audiobooks and music
  • Open-source software

− LIMITATIONS

  • Complex EPUB layouts limited
  • No DRM-protected files
  • Early release software

THE VERDICT

A promising choice for Linux users who prioritise offline access, privacy and straightforward reading and playback controls over advanced ebook rendering.
