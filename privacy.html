[OVERVIEW]

This is a small personal website created for fun.
I respect your privacy and collect as little information as possible.

[DATA_COLLECTION]

This website does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors.

However, some basic information may be collected automatically by the web hosting provider:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Date and time of visits

This information is used only for basic website operation and security.

[LOCAL_STORAGE]

Some pages use your browser's local storage to keep a visit count on that device. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to the site owner. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

[LISTENBRAINZ]

This site displays my own recent and currently playing music using public ListenBrainz data. This may include song titles, artists, albums and listening times. The information relates to my listening activity, not yours.

When a ListenBrainz widget or the listening journal loads, your browser makes requests directly to the ListenBrainz API. Like other external services, ListenBrainz may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the API endpoint requested. You do not need a ListenBrainz account to view this information, and my site does not send your listening history to ListenBrainz.

For more information, see the MetaBrainz privacy policy.

[TRAKT]

This site is designed to display my own television and film viewing history from Trakt, including titles, episodes and dates watched. A scheduled GitHub Actions process retrieves my public activity and saves a limited JSON snapshot for the About page and Now Watching journal. This is a cached record rather than a live feed.

Your browser normally loads that snapshot from this website, rather than contacting the Trakt API directly. You do not need a Trakt account to see it. Following a Trakt profile or title link will take you to Trakt, where its own privacy practices apply.

[THIRD_PARTY_MEDIA]

Some content on this site is loaded from other services. This includes ListenBrainz API requests, Google Fonts, external web-ring features, Webmention information, and potentially artwork from The Movie Database (TMDb).

If an image, font or other resource is served by another provider, your browser may connect to that provider and share ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser details and the page or resource requested. These providers have their own privacy practices. When a Trakt poster is hosted by TMDb rather than stored on this site, your browser may request it from TMDb directly.

[PUBLIC_ACTIVITY]

The listening and viewing information shown here comes from my own accounts and is activity I have chosen to make public. It does not represent your activity. You can browse these pages without signing in or linking any of your music or television accounts.

[EXTERNAL_LINKS]

This site may link to external websites such as music platforms, social networks, or other personal sites.
I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

[CHANGES]

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally if the site changes.

[CONTACT]

Questions? Use the contact links available on the website.
