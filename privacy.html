    [OVERVIEW]

    This is a small personal website created for fun.
    I respect your privacy and collect as little information as possible.

    [DATA_COLLECTION]

    This website does not intentionally collect personal information directly from visitors.

    However, some basic information may be processed automatically by the web hosting provider:

    • IP address
    • Browser type
    • Date and time of visits

    This information may be used for website operation, maintenance and security.

    [LOCAL_STORAGE]

    Some pages use your browser's local storage to keep a visit count on that device and remember your chosen light or dark theme. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to the site owner. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

    [LISTENBRAINZ]

    This website displays my own music listening activity using ListenBrainz, a project of the MetaBrainz Foundation.

    The information may include recently played tracks, artists, albums, listening times and whether a track is currently playing.

    My website retrieves this information from my public ListenBrainz profile. When these features load, your browser contacts the ListenBrainz API directly.

    As with other external web requests, ListenBrainz may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the API endpoint requested.

    You do not need a ListenBrainz account to view this information. My website does not collect your personal music listening history or submit listening information on your behalf.

    MetaBrainz Privacy Policy

    [TRAKT]

    This website also displays my own film and television viewing activity using Trakt.

    The information may include programme or film titles, episode details, artwork and viewing dates.

    The integration is designed to periodically retrieve my viewing activity and publish a limited snapshot as a JSON file on this website. Visitors can view this information without signing into Trakt or providing their own viewing history.

    The information may be cached and is not necessarily updated in real time.

    Trakt Privacy Policy

    [THIRD_PARTY_MEDIA]

    Some artwork and media information may come from external services, including The Movie Database (TMDb) and Cover Art Archive for album artwork.

    When your browser loads externally hosted artwork or other resources, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

    These services operate under their own privacy policies. My website does not use these integrations to collect your personal listening or viewing history.

    [OTHER_EXTERNAL_SERVICES]

    The Press Start 2P typeface is hosted locally on this website; no Google Fonts request is needed. Webmention.io may be contacted to display public replies, likes and reposts; externally hosted avatars may also load in your browser.

    The Webrings page embeds third-party scripts, stylesheets, images and frames. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when their widgets load. Their own privacy policies govern their processing. Third-party scripts may execute in the browser; I do not control their subsequent changes.

    The site uses Neocities hosting and Cloudflare for delivery and security. These services may process request information to operate and protect the site.

    [PUBLIC_ACTIVITY]

    The listening and viewing information displayed here is activity I have chosen to share publicly. It concerns my own accounts rather than those of visitors.

    You can browse the website without connecting your music or television accounts.
